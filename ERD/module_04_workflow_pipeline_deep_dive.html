quotations table

The entry point of the revenue pipeline. A quote tells the customer "here's what's wrong, here's what it costs, do you approve?" The current Dhalla system has a "Quotation" button in the nav but it's barely functional. In MechWise, quotes are the starting point for every job — even walk-ins get a verbal quote that's recorded in the system.

Quote status lifecycle
Draft — being built, not yet sent to customer. Lines can be freely added, edited, or removed. Only visible internally.
Sent — delivered to customer via SMS, email, or printed handout. Timestamp recorded. Quote is now read-only unless owner unlocks it for editing (which creates a new version: QT-0001-v2).
Approved — customer said yes. approved_date and approved_method recorded. "Convert to job card" button appears. This is the green light to start work.
Declined — customer said no. Reason field (optional): "Too expensive", "Going elsewhere", "Not now". Useful for win/loss analysis.
Expired — valid_until date has passed without approval. Auto-set by nightly job. System can auto-send a follow-up: "Your quote has expired. Would you like us to re-quote?"
Converted — approved quote has been converted into a job card. The quote_id is stored on the job card as a reference. No further changes allowed.
Business rules
SMS quote delivery: The quote is sent as a short message with a link to a web page showing the full quote details. e.g. "Hi Peter, your quote from Dhalla Automotive for Hyundai Tucson NSW-PL1 is $169.40. View details: mechwise.com.au/q/QT0001. Reply YES to approve." When the customer replies "YES", status auto-changes to Approved.
Email quote delivery: Generate a branded PDF with workshop logo, ABN, vehicle details, line items, and GST breakdown. Attach to email. Include an "Approve quote" button that links to the customer portal confirmation page.
Walk-in shortcut: When a car arrives without a prior quote, front desk creates a quote, immediately marks it "Approved" (method: In-Person), and converts to a job card in one flow. The system should support this "quick start" path — quote → approve → job card in 3 clicks.
Additional work discovered: If the mechanic finds extra problems during a job, a supplementary quote is created (linked to the original as "Additional quote for QT-0001"). This requires separate approval before the extra work begins. Never add unapproved work to an existing job card — it's a compliance and trust issue.
Edge cases
Multiple quotes for the same vehicle: A customer asks for pricing on three options — "just pads", "pads + rotors", "full brake service". Create 3 separate quotes. Only the approved one converts to a job card. The others auto-expire or are manually declined.
Quote revision: Customer wants to remove a line item or change a spec. Don't edit the sent quote — create a new version (QT-0001-v2) so there's an audit trail. The old version is marked "Superseded".
Insurance quotes: The insurer requires a specific format with assessor notes, photos, and a separate labour schedule. Support a "Quote type" field (Standard, Insurance, Fleet) that applies different PDF templates.
job_cards table

The heart of the workshop. A job card represents a vehicle that's in (or about to be in) the workshop being worked on. It tracks who's doing the work, what stage it's at, and what's been done. This is what appears on the workshop floor board and what mechanics interact with all day.

FieldTypeTagsValidation and notes
jobcard_idVARCHAR(10)PKAutoFormat: JC-0001. Sequential. This is the "job number" mechanics reference verbally — "I'm working on JC-fifteen".
quote_idVARCHAR(10)FKLinks back to the approved quote. NULL for walk-in jobs created without a prior quote (which still get an auto-generated internal quote for record-keeping).
client_idVARCHAR(10)FKRequiredInherited from quote or entered directly.
vehicle_idVARCHAR(10)FKRequiredInherited from quote or entered directly.
staff_idVARCHAR(10)FKPrimary mechanic assigned. Can be changed during the job. Individual line items can also have different mechanics (e.g. apprentice does oil change, senior mechanic does brakes).
date_inDATERequiredWhen the vehicle arrives / work begins. Defaults to today.
date_dueDATEPromised completion date. System calculates from estimated_duration and mechanic availability. If overdue, job card turns red on the floor board.
date_completedDATEAutoAuto-filled when status changes to "Completed". Used for turnaround time analytics.
mileage_inINTEGERValidatedOdometer reading at drop-off. Must be ≥ vehicle's last recorded mileage. Auto-updates the vehicle's current_mileage_km field. Required for service interval calculations.
bay_numberVARCHAR(20)Which workshop bay the vehicle is in. e.g. "Bay 1", "Bay 2", "Hoist 3", "Yard". Displayed on the floor board. Dhalla has ~4 bays.
statusVARCHAR(30)RequiredSee status lifecycle below. The core state machine of the platform.
priorityVARCHAR(10)Enum: Urgent, Normal, Low. Urgent jobs appear at the top of the floor board with a red border. Used for breakdowns, safety-critical repairs, and fleet vehicles that need same-day turnaround.
total_ex_gstDECIMAL(10,2)CalculatedSum of all job card line totals. May differ from the quote if extra work was added or prices adjusted.
internal_notesTEXTStaff-only notes. Not visible to customer. e.g. "Exhaust pipe rusting — mention to customer next visit", "Customer is difficult about pricing".
customer_notesTEXTVisible on the customer portal and printed on the invoice. e.g. "Recommend timing belt replacement at next service", "Monitor transmission fluid level".
Job card status lifecycle
Booked — scheduled for a future date. Vehicle hasn't arrived yet. Appears on the calendar view and daily schedule.
Waiting — vehicle has arrived but work hasn't started. Sitting in the yard or bay waiting for a mechanic. Clock starts ticking for turnaround time.
In progress — mechanic is actively working on the vehicle. Individual sub-items can be ticked off as completed. Bay is occupied.
Waiting for parts — work is paused because a required part is out of stock. Linked to a pending PO. System shows "Estimated: 2 days until part arrives" based on PO lead time. Bay may be freed for another vehicle.
Quality check — mechanic has finished, senior mechanic or owner is reviewing the work. Test drive, visual inspection, torque check. Optional step — can be skipped in workshop settings.
Ready for pickup — work is done, invoice generated, vehicle is parked and waiting for customer. SMS notification auto-sent: "Your vehicle is ready for collection." Payment can happen now or on pickup.
Completed — customer has collected the vehicle and payment received (or terms agreed). Job card is archived. date_completed filled. Maintenance history entry auto-created. Service reminder auto-scheduled.
Cancelled — job cancelled before or during work. If parts were deducted, they're returned to stock. Cancellation reason required. Partial-work scenarios generate a partial invoice.
Business rules
When status changes from "In progress" to "Waiting for parts", the system prompts: "Which part is needed?" Links to a PO or shows available stock. The bay can be reassigned to another vehicle while waiting.
When status changes to "Ready for pickup", auto-send SMS to the client: "Hi [name], your [make model] ([rego]) is ready for collection at Dhalla Automotive. Total: $[amount]. Open [hours]." Configurable template in workshop settings.
Additional work during a job: Mechanic discovers worn brake pads during a minor service. The system enforces: (1) create a supplementary quote, (2) contact customer for approval, (3) only then add the extra lines to the job card. Never let a mechanic add unapproved work — it causes disputes at pickup.
Turnaround tracking: date_in to date_completed = total turnaround. Target is same-day for minor services, 1–2 days for major work. Dashboard shows average turnaround by job type and flags overdue jobs.
Edge cases
Vehicle left overnight: Customer drops off at 4:30pm, work starts next morning. date_in is today (drop-off), but actual work hours are tracked separately on job card lines. Don't count overnight as work time.
Split job across mechanics: Oil change done by the apprentice (ST-005), brakes done by the senior mechanic (ST-001). Each job card line has its own staff_id. The header staff_id shows the primary/responsible mechanic.
Customer changes mind mid-job: Approved the full brake service but calls back saying "just do the pads, skip the rotors." The job card line for rotor machining is removed (or marked "Customer declined"). Quote revised and price adjusted before invoicing.
invoices table

The billing document. Generated from a completed job card — line items flow directly from job card lines to invoice lines without re-entry. The invoice is the legal tax document that satisfies ATO requirements and goes into accounting.

FieldTypeTagsValidation and notes

jobcard_idVARCHAR(10)FKLinks to the completed job card. One-to-one: each job card generates exactly one invoice. NULL for manual invoices (rare — e.g. selling parts over the counter without a job card).
client_idVARCHAR(10)FKRequiredWho is being billed.
vehicle_idVARCHAR(10)FKRequiredWhich vehicle. Rego, make, model, year, VIN all appear on the invoice PDF.
invoice_dateDATEAutoDefaults to today (date of completion). Editable for end-of-month billing scenarios.
due_dateDATEAuto= invoice_date + payment terms. Default: same day (payment on pickup). Fleet accounts may have 14 or 30 day terms. Overdue invoices auto-flagged after this date.
tax_typeVARCHAR(20)"GST Inclusive" (default). "GST Exclusive" for business-to-business where client handles their own GST. "GST Free" for exempt items.
subtotal_ex_gstDECIMAL(10,2)CalculatedSum of all line item totals (ex-GST).
gst_amountDECIMAL(10,2)CalculatedSum of all line item GST amounts. Calculated per-line, not on the header — ensures accuracy when lines have different GST rates.

Invoice PDF contents (ATO-compliant tax invoice)
Header: Workshop logo, business name, ABN, MVRL/ARC numbers, address, phone, email.
Customer block: Client name (or business name), address, phone, email.
Vehicle block: Registration, make, model, year, VIN, odometer reading, fuel type, transmission.
Service block: Next service due (date + km), pink slip expiry, reading at service, next service reading.
Line items: S.No, Description, Parts used, Qty, Unit price (ex-GST), Line total. Grouped by parent job name.
Totals: Subtotal ex-GST, Discount (if any), GST amount, Total inc-GST. Payment method and reference.
Footer: "Thank you for choosing Dhalla Automotive. Your next service is due [date] or at [km] km."
Business rules
Sequential numbering is legally required. The system must never skip, reuse, or delete an invoice number. Voided invoices keep their number and appear as "VOID" in the sequence.
next_service_due auto-triggers: (1) update the vehicle's next_service_due field, (2) create a service_reminder record with status "Pending", (3) schedule SMS/email reminders at 30/14/7 days before due.
Invoice editing: Once an invoice is marked "Paid", it cannot be edited — only voided and re-issued. This prevents after-the-fact tampering. Unpaid invoices can be edited by Owner or Manager with an audit log entry.
payments table

Tracks every dollar that moves in or out. Separated from invoices to support partial payments, multiple payment methods on one invoice, and supplier payments. This is the foundation for cash flow reporting and BAS lodgement.

Business rules
When a customer payment is recorded against an invoice: compare sum of all payments for that invoice_id against invoice.final_amount. If equal: set payment_status = "Paid". If less: set payment_status = "Partial" and calculate outstanding balance.
Daily cash reconciliation: Sum all payment_method = "Cash" payments for today = expected cash in till. Owner counts physical cash and enters the actual. Difference is recorded as "Cash variance" — positive (over) or negative (short).
Every payment auto-creates an accounting_ledger entry. Customer payments = Incoming. Supplier and Expense payments = Outgoing. GST is extracted from the related invoice or PO for BAS reporting.
Edge cases
Bounced payment: Customer's card is declined after the vehicle has been released. Create a reversal payment (negative amount) and set invoice back to "Unpaid". Flag the client record: "Previous payment issue — request payment before release."
Overpayment: Customer pays $500 on a $495 invoice. Record full $500 payment. System shows $5 credit balance on the client account. Apply to next invoice or refund.
Contra/barter: Customer is a plumber who does workshop plumbing in exchange for car servicing. Record as a payment with method "Contra" and a notes field explaining the arrangement. Amount still reflects the invoice value for GST purposes.
Line items — the shared structure

Quotes, job cards, and invoices all share the same line item structure. This is deliberate — data flows through the pipeline without re-entry. When a quote is approved, its lines are copied to the job card. When the job is completed, job card lines are copied to the invoice. Same fields, same format, same traceability.

Data flow between stages
Quote lines
→ copy
Job card lines
→ copy
Invoice lines
Copy, not move: Each stage has its own line records. The quote lines are preserved as the "what was promised." The job card lines show "what was actually done" (with actual_labour_hrs and possible additions). The invoice lines are the final "what was billed." If a discrepancy exists between what was quoted and what was billed, the system flags it for owner review.
Line items can be added to a job card that weren't on the original quote (discovered extra work). These are flagged as "Additional — not on original quote" and require a supplementary approval. They flow to the invoice marked as supplementary.
