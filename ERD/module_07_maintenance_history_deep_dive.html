maintenance_history table

The permanent, immutable record of everything that's ever been done to a vehicle at this workshop. Auto-generated when a job card is completed — one history entry per completed job. This is what the mechanic checks before starting work ("when was the timing belt last done?") and what the customer sees on their portal.

FieldTypeTagsValidation and notes

vehicle_idVARCHAR(10)FKRequiredWhich vehicle. The history stays with the vehicle even if ownership changes — a new buyer can see the full service record.
jobcard_idVARCHAR(10)FKLinks to the job card that generated this record. Click through to see full detail: line items, mechanic notes, parts used.

Auto-generation rules
When a job card's status changes to "Completed", the system auto-creates a maintenance_history record by pulling: service_date from date_completed, service_type by concatenating parent job names, description by listing all completed sub-items, mileage from mileage_in, total_cost from the linked invoice's final_amount, and notes from both customer_notes and internal_notes fields.
The record is immutable once created — no edits, no deletes. If a correction is needed (wrong mileage entered, wrong part noted), the correction is logged as an addendum to the existing record with a timestamp and staff_id, not an overwrite.
Business rules
When a mechanic opens a new job card for a vehicle, the system shows a sidebar with the last 5 maintenance history records. This gives instant context: "Last service was 6 months ago at 92,500km — minor service. Before that: brakes at 85,000km."
Pre-purchase inspection scenario: A buyer asks "what's been done to this car?" The workshop can print or email a full vehicle history report — every service, every part, every mileage reading. This is a value-add service the workshop can charge for ($20–$50) and builds trust in the platform.
Edge cases
Service done elsewhere: The customer says "I had the timing belt done at another workshop 6 months ago." The system should support a "Manual history entry" — staff enters the date, description, mileage, and notes with a flag "Reported by customer — not verified." This prevents the mechanic from recommending work that's already been done.
Warranty claims: If a part fails within warranty (e.g. alternator dies 3 months after installation), the history record for the original installation is the proof. The system should support linking a warranty job card back to the original: "Warranty replacement — ref MH-0034 (alternator installed 12 Jul 2025)."

Business rules
The timeline is the first thing shown when a vehicle is selected anywhere in the system (job card, quote, appointment). It provides instant context without clicking through to separate screens.
Mechanic recommendations (customer_notes) from previous visits should be highlighted on new job cards. If the last visit noted "rear pads at 40%", and the car is back 6 months later, the system surfaces: "Previous recommendation: rear brake pads were at 40%. Check and quote if needed."
Customer portal

A simple, read-mostly web page where customers can see their vehicle's service history, upcoming service dates, approve quotes, and view invoices. Reduces phone calls ("when is my service due?"), builds trust, and provides a digital touchpoint for the workshop brand. Access via a unique link sent by SMS — no app install, no password required initially.

Portal access model
Phase 1 — Magic link: SMS contains a unique URL: mechwise.au/p/abc123. No login required — the link itself is the authentication. Valid for 30 days, refreshed each time a new SMS is sent. This removes all friction — the customer taps the link and sees their info.
Phase 2 — Login: Optional email/password account for customers who want persistent access. Google/Apple sign-in for convenience. Required for fleet managers who need to view multiple vehicles.

Business rules
The portal shows customer_notes from job cards but never internal_notes. e.g. "Recommend timing belt at next service" is visible; "Customer argued about the brake price" is not.
Invoice PDFs are downloadable — customers need these for insurance claims, fleet management, and tax deductions (for business vehicles). The download link stays active indefinitely.
The portal URL is unique per client, not per vehicle. If Peter has two vehicles, both appear on the same portal page. Fleet managers see all fleet vehicles on one dashboard.
Data completeness tracking

The current Dhalla database has massive gaps — 0% email capture, most vehicles have only a rego number, many phone numbers are malformed. Rather than forcing a one-time cleanup (impossible for 300+ clients), MechWise tracks completeness and prompts for missing data at natural touchpoints.

Business rules
Completeness scores are shown on the dashboard as a progress widget. The goal is to reach 80%+ within 3 months of going live — achieved naturally through daily use, not data entry marathons.
Never block a workflow because data is missing. A quote can be created without a client email. A job card can be created without a VIN. The system works with what it has and asks for more at the right time.
Weekly completeness report email to the workshop owner: "This week: 12 emails captured, 8 vehicle details auto-filled, 15 parts priced. Overall data completeness: 34% → 41%. Top missing: client emails (240 remaining)."
Audit trail

An immutable log of every create, update, and delete action in the system. Required for ATO compliance (7-year retention), dispute resolution, and security monitoring. Staff can't see the audit trail — it's owner-only and designed for accountability.

audit_log table
FieldTypeNotes
log_idBIGINT AUTO_INCREMENTPK Numeric, never gaps, never resets.
timestampTIMESTAMPExact time of the action, UTC. Displayed in workshop timezone on the UI.
user_idVARCHAR(10)FK Who performed the action. Links to users table.
actionVARCHAR(20)Enum: CREATE, UPDATE, DELETE, LOGIN, LOGOUT, EXPORT, VOID, PAYMENT.
table_nameVARCHAR(50)Which table was affected. e.g. "invoices", "job_cards", "clients".
record_idVARCHAR(20)The PK of the affected record. e.g. "INV-0089", "CL-005".
field_nameVARCHAR(50)For UPDATE actions: which field changed. NULL for CREATE/DELETE.
old_valueTEXTThe value before the change. NULL for CREATE.
new_valueTEXTThe value after the change. NULL for DELETE.
ip_addressVARCHAR(45)IP address of the user. Useful for identifying suspicious access from unknown locations.
What gets logged
Every invoice creation, edit, void, and payment. Every price change on parts or job sub-items. Every client or vehicle record modification. Every login and logout. Every data export. Every user permission change.
The audit log is append-only and write-protected — no user (not even the owner) can edit or delete audit entries. In a database where the owner has admin access, the audit table should be on a separate schema or have row-level security preventing DELETE/UPDATE.
Retention and compliance
ATO requires business records to be kept for 5 years (7 years for some). The audit log, combined with the accounting ledger and invoice records, satisfies this requirement. The system should prevent deletion of any financial record within the retention period.
Edge cases
Bulk operations: An owner imports 300 clients from CSV. This generates 300 CREATE entries in the audit log. The system should batch these with a single parent log entry: "Bulk import: 300 clients created from file 'dhalla_customers.csv'" plus individual child entries for traceability.
Vehicle ownership transfer

What happens when a vehicle changes hands — sold, gifted, repossessed, or traded in. The service history stays with the vehicle (it's a property of the VIN, not the owner), but reminders and invoicing switch to the new owner.

Transfer workflow
1. Front desk opens the vehicle record and clicks "Transfer ownership."
2. System shows current owner and asks: "Who is the new owner?" Option to select existing client or create new.
3. Current client_vehicles link: ownership_end = today, is_primary_owner = FALSE.
4. New client_vehicles link created: ownership_start = today, is_primary_owner = TRUE.
5. All pending service_reminders for this vehicle: cancelled for old owner, recreated for new owner (if new owner has a phone/email).
6. Vehicle timeline receives a grey "Ownership change" event: "Transferred from Peter (CL-005) to [new owner]."
7. Old owner's portal still shows historical records for this vehicle (greyed out, marked "No longer owned") but no future reminders.
Transfer scenarios
ScenarioHandling
Sold to a new customerNew customer brings the car for a service and says "I just bought this." Create new client, transfer ownership. Full service history is now available to the new owner and mechanic.
Sold privately, buyer unknownOld owner calls and says "I sold the car." End their ownership. Vehicle goes into "Unassigned" status. If the new owner never shows up, the vehicle eventually becomes inactive.
Fleet vehicle reassignedSame fleet client, but the driver changes. The client_vehicles link stays (fleet is still the owner). Update the driver contact details on the client record or add a new "Authorised driver" link.
Trade-in at a dealershipSame as "sold privately." The workshop likely won't see this car again unless the new owner happens to bring it in.
Inherited or giftedNew client_vehicles link with relationship = "Family Member" converted to "Owner". Ownership_start = date of transfer.
Business rules
Service history is permanent and vehicle-bound. Ownership changes affect who gets contacted (reminders, invoices) but never alter the historical record. This is essential for pre-purchase inspections — the buyer needs to see what was done regardless of who owned the car at the time.
When a transferred vehicle returns with a new owner, the system should flag: "This vehicle was previously serviced under [old owner]. 5 historical records available." The mechanic gets full context even though the customer is new.
