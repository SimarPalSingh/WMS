accounting_ledger table

A unified transaction record of every dollar that moves through the workshop. Auto-populated — when an invoice is paid, a ledger entry is created. When a PO is received, another entry appears. When rent or electricity is paid, the owner logs an expense. This is the single source of truth for all financial reporting.

Auto-population rules
Invoice paid → TXN created: type=Invoice, direction=Incoming, entity=Customer, amounts from invoice totals, related_doc=INV-xxxx.
PO received → TXN created: type=Purchase, direction=Outgoing, entity=Supplier, amounts from PO totals, related_doc=PO-xxxx.
Supplier payment made → TXN created: type=Payment, direction=Outgoing, entity=Supplier, amounts from payment record, related_doc=PAY-xxxx.
Refund issued → TXN created: type=Refund, direction=Outgoing, entity=Customer (same client), amounts from refund payment record.
Manual expense → Owner enters: type=Expense, direction=Outgoing, entity_name="Rent" or "Electricity" etc. Manual entry with receipt upload in phase 2.
Business rules
The ledger is append-only. Entries are never edited or deleted — corrections are made by adding a reversing entry. This ensures the audit trail is complete and tamper-proof. If an invoice is voided, a matching reversing TXN is created (same amounts, opposite direction).
Timing: Ledger entries are created when money moves (cash basis) by default. For workshops using accrual accounting (recognising revenue when the invoice is issued, not when paid), the system creates the entry on invoice date and updates payment_status when payment arrives. The BAS tab explains which method to use.
BAS and GST compliance

Every GST-registered Australian business must lodge a Business Activity Statement (BAS) quarterly. This is the single most painful administrative task for workshop owners — tallying up GST collected on sales, GST paid on purchases, and calculating the net amount owed to the ATO. MechWise automates this entirely.

Australian FY runs July to June. Most small workshops lodge quarterly. Annual turnover under $10M can use the simpler "Simpler BAS" which only reports total sales, GST on sales, GST on purchases, and net GST.

Cash vs accrual accounting
Cash basis (recommended for workshops under $10M turnover): GST is reported when money changes hands. Invoice issued in December but paid in January → GST counts in Q3, not Q2. Simpler, matches bank statements. MechWise default.
Accrual basis: GST is reported when the invoice is issued, regardless of payment. Invoice issued in December → GST counts in Q2 even if unpaid. Required for larger businesses. MechWise supports both — configurable in workshop settings.
The accounting_method setting (Cash or Accrual) changes which date the ledger uses for BAS period assignment. Cash: uses payment_date from the payment record. Accrual: uses invoice_date from the invoice record.
BAS lodgement workflow
1. At quarter end, the system auto-generates the BAS summary. Dashboard shows a banner: "Q2 BAS is ready for review. Due: 28 Feb."
2. Owner reviews the figures. The system shows any unreconciled items: unpaid invoices (accrual method), missing supplier invoices, expenses without receipts.
3. Owner exports the BAS summary as a PDF or CSV. Sends to their accountant or enters directly into the ATO Business Portal / myGov.
4. Owner marks the quarter as "Lodged" in MechWise. System records the lodgement date and archives the quarter.
Edge cases
GST-free items: Some insurance work and government contracts are GST-free. These must be excluded from GST calculations but still included in total sales (G1). The gst_amount for these lines is $0, but amount_ex_gst still contributes to revenue.
Adjustments: If a previously reported invoice is voided in a later quarter, the adjustment appears in the current quarter's BAS. The system handles this by creating a reversing ledger entry with a negative amount in the current period.
Mixed-method invoices: An invoice with both GST-inclusive items (standard parts and labour) and GST-free items (certain insurance repairs). Per-line GST rates handle this — the invoice header sums the mixed GST correctly.

Business rules
All KPIs show a comparison to the previous period (last month, last quarter, same quarter last year). Green arrow = improving, red = declining, grey = stable (within 2% variance).
Profitability KPIs are only visible to users with can_view_financials = TRUE. Mechanics see only the operations KPIs relevant to their work (turnaround, jobs completed).
KPIs are computed in real-time from the ledger and source tables — not stored as static values. This means they're always current but may be slower on large datasets. Phase 2 adds a nightly rollup table for historical KPIs to improve performance.

Business rules
All reports support date range filtering, PDF export, and CSV export. PDF generates a branded document with workshop logo suitable for handing to the accountant. CSV is for further analysis in Excel.
Mechanic productivity and P&L reports are restricted to Owner role. Sharing these with staff requires explicit opt-in in workshop settings.
Report caching: Reports on the current period are computed live. Historical reports (closed months and quarters) are cached and served instantly. If the source data changes (late invoice, correction), the cache is invalidated and regenerated.
Export and accounting software integration

Most workshop owners don't do their own bookkeeping — they have an accountant who uses Xero, MYOB, or QuickBooks. MechWise doesn't try to replace the accountant's software. Instead, it produces clean export files that the accountant can import directly.

Accounting software integration roadmap
Phase 1: CSV export. Manual process — owner downloads CSV from MechWise, sends to accountant, accountant imports into Xero/MYOB. Simple, reliable, no API integration needed.
Phase 2: Xero API integration. Direct push of invoices, payments, and expenses to Xero via their API. Auto-sync: when an invoice is created in MechWise, it appears in Xero within minutes. Two-way: payments recorded in Xero can update MechWise. Xero covers ~60% of Australian small businesses.
Phase 3: MYOB and QuickBooks. Same as Xero but via MYOB AccountRight API and QuickBooks Online API. Covers the remaining ~30% of accountants.
Chart of accounts mapping
When integrating with accounting software, MechWise transactions need to map to the accountant's chart of accounts. Default mapping: service revenue → "Sales — Mechanical repairs" (4-1000), parts revenue → "Sales — Parts" (4-1100), parts purchases → "Cost of goods — Parts" (5-1000), labour cost → "Wages — Mechanical" (6-1000), expenses → mapped per category. The workshop owner or accountant configures this mapping once in settings.
Edge cases
Dual-entry prevention: If Xero integration is active, the accountant shouldn't also be manually entering invoices from emailed PDFs. The system should include a "Connected to Xero" banner on exported PDFs: "This invoice is auto-synced to your accounting software. Do not enter manually."
Bank reconciliation: MechWise knows which invoices were paid by card vs cash vs transfer, but it doesn't see bank transactions. Reconciliation happens in the accounting software. MechWise's daily takings report provides the expected figures for the accountant to match against bank statements.
