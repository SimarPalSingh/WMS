workshops table

The root entity. Everything in MechWise belongs to a workshop. This enables multi-tenancy from day one — when you sell to a second customer, their data is completely isolated.

Business rules
ABN must be provided before any invoice can be generated. The system should warn on setup if ABN is missing: "Add your ABN to start invoicing."
Workshop hours drive the appointment booking calendar — time slots outside operating hours should be greyed out and unbookable.
Multi-workshop: When workshop_id is present on every table, a franchise owner can switch between locations in the same login. Data never leaks across workshops.
Edge cases
Some workshops operate from home without a formal shopfront. Address is still required for invoice compliance, but "Garage_Location" on vehicles should be optional.
Mobile workshops (those that go to the customer) may not have a fixed address. Support a "Mobile" checkbox that replaces address with a service area description.
clients table

Separated from vehicles. One client can own 1, 5, or 50 vehicles. The client record captures who pays and who to contact — the vehicle record captures what gets serviced.

FieldTypeTagsValidation and notes
client_idVARCHAR(10)PKAutoFormat: CL-001. Auto-incremented.
client_typeVARCHAR(20)RequiredEnum: "Individual" or "Business". Drives which name fields are shown — Individual shows first/last, Business shows business_name.
business_nameVARCHAR(200)Required when client_type = "Business". Used on invoices. e.g. "Revo Kitchens Pty Ltd", "Akay Enterprises Aus Pty Ltd"
first_nameVARCHAR(50)Required when client_type = "Individual". Minimum 2 characters.
last_nameVARCHAR(50)Optional — many existing customers only have first names ("Kuku", "Jassi", "Bittu").
mobileVARCHAR(20)ValidatedAustralian mobile: must start with 04 and be 10 digits. Auto-format on save: "0430117807" becomes "0430 117 807". Strips spaces/dashes on input.
landlineVARCHAR(20)ValidatedAustralian landline: starts with 02/03/07/08, 10 digits total.
emailVARCHAR(150)ValidatedNewStandard email regex. Currently 0% populated — the system prompts for email at every touchpoint: quote approval, invoice delivery, booking confirmation.
abnVARCHAR(20)ValidatedNewOnly for business clients. ABN checksum validation. Enables accurate BAS reporting when client is also a supplier.
addressVARCHAR(200)NewStreet address. Optional but needed for mailing invoices and for insurance claims.
suburbVARCHAR(100)
stateVARCHAR(10)Dropdown. Defaults to NSW.
postcodeVARCHAR(10)Validated4-digit Australian postcode.
preferred_contactVARCHAR(20)NewEnum: SMS | Email | Phone | WhatsApp. Determines how automated reminders are sent. Default: SMS (most mechanics prefer texting).

is_activeBOOLEANSoft delete. Inactive clients don't appear in search or dropdowns but their history is preserved.
Business rules
At least one of mobile, landline, or email must be provided. A client with no contact method can't receive reminders, quotes, or invoices.
Duplicate detection on save: if another client has the same mobile number, show a warning: "A client with this number already exists: [Name]. Link this vehicle to them instead?" This prevents the Satnam Randava x3 problem.
Progressive data capture: The first time a client visits, you might only have a name and phone. The system shouldn't block on missing fields — instead, it shows a "Profile 40% complete" bar and prompts for email/address at natural touchpoints (sending a quote, generating an invoice).
Edge cases
Same person, different phones: "Simon" appears with 0466286977 (DQB61Y), 0469700288 (DN24GZ and AY81BW), and 0402110194 (SML034). Might be same or different people. The system should flag but not auto-merge — let the front desk decide.
Business clients with authorised contacts: "Harpal Kahlon Pty Ltd" might send different drivers each time. Support a "contacts" sub-list on business accounts — multiple names/phones tied to the same CL-ID.
Privacy: Client data is PII under Australian Privacy Act. The system must support data export (right of access) and data deletion (right to erasure) requests. Soft delete first, hard delete after 7-year retention period for tax records.
vehicles table

The thing that gets serviced. Separated from the client who owns it. Currently 323 vehicles with only rego numbers — all other fields are empty. The rego lookup API is the key to filling this automatically.

FieldTypeTagsValidation and notes
vehicle_idVARCHAR(10)PKAutoFormat: VH-001. Auto-incremented.
registration_noVARCHAR(20)RequiredValidatedMust be unique per workshop. Auto-uppercase on input. Strip spaces. NSW regos are typically 6 chars (e.g. "DFG45Z") but custom plates vary ("02REVO", "BADWAL", "MRKANG").
stateVARCHAR(10)Which state the vehicle is registered in. Defaults to NSW. Determines which rego lookup API to call.
make_idVARCHAR(10)FKAutoLinks to vehicle_makes lookup. Auto-populated by rego lookup API. Dropdown with type-ahead search for manual entry.
vehicle_modelVARCHAR(50)AutoAuto-populated by rego lookup. e.g. "Tucson", "Corolla", "CRV".
vehicle_yearINTEGERAutoValidated4-digit year. Must be between 1950 and current year + 1. Auto-populated by rego lookup.
vehicle_colorVARCHAR(30)AutoAuto-populated. Standard color names: White, Black, Silver, Grey, Red, Blue, etc.
body_typeVARCHAR(30)NewEnum: Sedan, SUV, Hatch, Ute, Van, Wagon, Coupe, Convertible, Truck, Bus, Trailer.
vinVARCHAR(20)AutoValidatedVehicle Identification Number. 17 characters. Must pass VIN checksum (position 9 check digit). Auto-populated by rego lookup. Critical for warranty claims and part ordering.
engine_noVARCHAR(30)NewStamped on engine block. Useful for insurance claims and theft recovery. Manual entry only.
engine_capacityVARCHAR(20)Autoe.g. "2.0L", "3.5L", "Electric". Auto-populated.
fuel_typeVARCHAR(20)AutoEnum: Petrol, Diesel, LPG, LPG/Petrol, Electric, Hybrid, Hydrogen. Determines which oil types and service kits apply.
transmissionVARCHAR(10)AutoEnum: Auto, Manual, CVT, DCT. Determines transmission fluid type and clutch applicability.
current_mileage_kmINTEGERValidatedUpdated each time vehicle is serviced (from job card mileage_in field). Must never decrease — if a lower value is entered, show warning: "Mileage is lower than last recorded. Check odometer reading."
pink_slip_expiryDATENSW e-safety check expiry. Auto-calculated: last inspection date + 6 months (or 12 months for newer vehicles). Drives Pink Slip reminder alerts.
next_service_dueDATEAutoAuto-populated from the latest invoice's next_service_due field. Drives Service Reminder alerts.
next_service_kmINTEGERAutoAuto-populated from invoice. e.g. current 92,500km + 10,000km interval = 102,500km.
Business rules
Rego lookup API workflow: (1) User types rego, (2) system calls TfNSW/NEVDIS API, (3) auto-fills make, model, year, VIN, color, fuel, transmission, engine capacity, (4) user confirms or edits. This alone saves 2-3 minutes per new vehicle entry.
When mileage is updated via a job card, auto-check: if current_mileage_km is within 1,000km of next_service_km, trigger a "service due soon" notification.
Electric vehicles: fuel_type = "Electric" should hide oil-related service items from the job catalogue picker. No oil filter, no engine oil, no spark plugs. Different service schedule.
Edge cases
Unregistered vehicles: Trailers, farm equipment, or vehicles being repaired before re-registration may not have a valid rego. Allow a "No Rego" or placeholder entry but still require a VIN or description.
Interstate vehicles: A VIC-registered car serviced in NSW. The rego lookup API differs by state. Support state selection before rego lookup.
Personalised plates: "02REVO", "MRKANG", "BADWAL" won't follow standard rego patterns. Don't reject based on format — let any alphanumeric string through.
client_vehicles table (junction)

The relationship bridge. This is what makes the data model flexible — one client can own many vehicles, one vehicle can have ownership history, and fleet companies work properly.

FieldTypeTagsValidation and notes
link_idVARCHAR(10)PKAutoFormat: LK-001.
client_idVARCHAR(10)FKRequiredReferences clients.client_id. Cascading soft-delete: if client is deactivated, links remain for history.
vehicle_idVARCHAR(10)FKRequiredReferences vehicles.vehicle_id.
ownership_startDATEWhen the client acquired this vehicle. Optional but useful for service history context.
ownership_endDATENULL = currently owned. Filled when vehicle is sold or transferred. Past-owned vehicles still show in client history but are greyed out.
is_primary_ownerBOOLEANOnly one primary owner per vehicle at any time. The primary owner is who gets service reminders and invoices. Enforced by DB constraint.
relationshipVARCHAR(30)Enum: Owner, Authorised Driver, Fleet Manager, Family Member. Fleet Manager can approve quotes and view all fleet vehicles.
Business rules
When a new vehicle is created, auto-create a client_vehicles link with is_primary_owner = TRUE and ownership_start = today.
When searching for a vehicle by rego, show the current primary owner's details (name, phone) alongside it. If the person dropping off is different, offer: "Is [name] still the owner? Or link to a different client."
Fleet scenario: "Insite Care" (CL-XXX) owns both 718JC2 and 2BN5ST. When front desk types either rego, the system shows "Insite Care" as the client and lists all fleet vehicles in a sidebar. The fleet manager can view service history and costs across all vehicles.
Edge cases
Vehicle sold to another existing client: Set ownership_end on old link, create new link with new client. Service reminders switch to the new owner. Old owner's history for that vehicle remains accessible but marked as "previous owner".
Shared vehicles (family car): Two clients can both be linked to the same vehicle — e.g. husband and wife. One is primary (gets reminders), the other is "Family Member" (can drop off and approve work).
Vehicle with no known owner (e.g. tow-in, insurance job): Create a placeholder client "Unknown / Insurance Job" and link. Update when the real owner is identified.
staff table

Everyone who works at the workshop. Not just mechanics — also front desk, apprentices, and the owner. Staff records drive job assignment, time tracking, labour costing, and the workshop floor board.

FieldTypeTagsValidation and notes
staff_idVARCHAR(10)PKAutoFormat: ST-001.
first_nameVARCHAR(50)RequiredMinimum 2 characters.
last_nameVARCHAR(50)Optional — some existing mechanics are single-name ("Ash", "Smile", "Joen").
roleVARCHAR(30)RequiredEnum: Owner/Mechanic, Manager, Mechanic, Apprentice, Front Desk, Read-Only. Determines default permissions when creating a user account.
mobileVARCHAR(20)ValidatedFor rostering notifications and emergency contact.
hourly_rateDECIMAL(8,2)NewInternal cost rate. NOT the rate charged to customers (that's the job_sub_items.labour_rate). Used for profitability analysis: if mechanic costs $35/hr and you charge $80/hr, gross margin on labour is 56%.
employment_typeVARCHAR(20)NewEnum: Full-Time, Part-Time, Casual, Contractor. Affects availability scheduling and leave tracking.
certificationsVARCHAR(200)NewComma-separated: "MVRL, ARC, ASE". Pink slip inspections require MVRL certification — the system should only allow MVRL-certified staff to be assigned to pink slip jobs.
availability_daysVARCHAR(50)e.g. "Mon-Fri", "Mon-Sat", "Wed,Thu,Fri". Drives the appointment scheduler — can't book a mechanic on their day off.
availability_hrsVARCHAR(50)e.g. "7:30AM-5:00PM", "8:00AM-4:30PM". Drives time slot availability in booking calendar.
Business rules
When assigning a mechanic to a job card, the dropdown should only show staff who are: (a) available on the job date, (b) have the right certifications for that job type, and (c) are not already at capacity (check existing job cards for that day).
Hourly rate is hidden from all roles except Owner and Manager. Mechanics should not see each other's pay rates.
Apprentice supervision: If a job is assigned to an Apprentice, the system should require a supervising Mechanic to be co-assigned. This is an Australian legal requirement for apprentice work.
Edge cases
Owner doing everything: In Dhalla's case, Tinku is owner, mechanic, and front desk. His staff record should have role "Owner/Mechanic" with full permissions, but the system should still support proper separation when he hires dedicated front desk staff.
Subcontractors: A mobile auto-electrician or tow truck driver who does occasional work. Employment_type "Contractor" — they don't appear on the roster but can be assigned to specific jobs.
users table

Authentication and access control. Linked to staff but separate — a staff member exists even without a login, and you might have system-level users (like an admin account) that aren't tied to a specific staff member.

Business rules
Session timeout: 8 hours for workshop staff (they're logged in all day). 30 minutes for customer portal.
Audit log: Every create, update, and delete action records who, when, and what changed. Stored in a separate audit_log table. Retained for 7 years (ATO requirement).
Current system uses "dhalla@tinku" as username with a simple password. Migration should force a password reset for all users on first login to the new platform.
